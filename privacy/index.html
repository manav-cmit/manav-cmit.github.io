Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026. This policy covers the Duo Party Android app on Google Play (package com.dev.secretearn).

Duo Party is a collection of free games: two-player games, board games and solo arcade games. Most games work offline; online board-game rooms need the internet. The app has no real-money rewards, no payments and no in-app purchases.

What we collect

  • Date of birth you enter when you first open the app: only to confirm you are 18 or over. It is checked and kept on your phone only; it is not sent to us. Phones that enter an under-18 date are blocked.
  • Older versions (1.9.5 and earlier) sent the date of birth, the device ID (Android ID) and the IP address to our server for the age check. Version 1.9.6 and later no longer do. You can still ask us to delete that data (see below).
  • Online rooms (optional): if you play a board game online with friends, our server receives the nickname you type, a random id for your phone and the moves of that game, so every phone in the room sees the same board. Your nickname is shown only to the people in that room. Rude nicknames are replaced with "Player". A room and everything in it is deleted 6 hours after its last move.

Game data (scores, levels, stars, play tokens, settings and badges) is stored only on your phone and never sent to us. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob (Google LLC). Google may collect your advertising ID, device and usage information to show and measure ads, under its own privacy policy. See how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Where the law requires it (in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Watching an optional video ad gives in-game items only (such as a shield or a revive), never money or points. Play tokens counted while you play are just for fun: they stay on your phone and have no cash value.

How we use data

  • Check, on your phone, that you are 18 or over.
  • Run online board-game rooms you choose to join.

We do not sell your personal data and do not share it with anyone except the ad partner named above.

Storage and retention

Data is stored on Cloudflare servers, sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Online rooms are deleted 6 hours after their last move. Data from older versions is kept until you ask us to delete it.

Delete your data

To delete the data stored on our server, email secretearn.help@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". We delete any date of birth, device ID and related records we hold within 30 days. Uninstalling the app removes the game data stored on your phone.

Children

Duo Party is for adults aged 18 or over. The app asks for your date of birth before anything else, and under-18s cannot play. The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has used the app, email us and we will delete the data.

Changes and contact

We will post changes on this page. For questions or deletion requests, email secretearn.help@gmail.com. See also our Terms of Service.
