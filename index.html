Duo Party: 107 Games in 1

A free games app for Android by Stellorin (package com.dev.secretearn).

Get it on Google Play

What's inside

  • 89 two-player games on one phone: racing, fighting, sports, ships, cards and dice, brain games and more. Every game also has a computer opponent and 10 levels.
  • 6 board games: Ludo, Snakes & Ladders, Chess, Carrom, Pool and Tic-Tac-Toe.
  • Play online with friends: create a room in Ludo, Chess, Snakes & Ladders or Tic-Tac-Toe, share the link or 6-letter code, and play on your own phones.
  • 12 solo games, including 3D road racing: beat your best score.
  • Token miner: a counter that grows while you play. Tokens are just for fun and have no cash value.

Good to know

  • For players aged 18 and over.
  • Free with ads. There are no payments, no in-app purchases and no real-money rewards.
